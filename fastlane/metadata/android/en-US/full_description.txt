Battery Status shows what your battery and charger are really doing, refreshed every second.

Without any special permission it reads the Android BatteryManager: level, charging state, current, voltage, power in watts, temperature and time to full.

Grant it Shizuku access (wireless debugging or root) and it also reads dumpsys battery and every /sys/class/power_supply node as the shell user:

• Negotiated charger current/voltage/power, USB type (SDP/DCP/PD), Type-C PD capabilities, charge phase
• Design capacity vs. learned full capacity, capacity health %, cycle count
• Vendor health index, internal resistance, manufacturing date (where the driver exposes them)
• Raw dumpsys, uevent and sysfs dumps, plus a list of sysfs nodes the app does not yet understand

A diagnostics report (device info, raw data, in-app log) can be shared or saved from the settings sheet to help improve support for your device. No network access, no analytics.
